Abilities
------
This bundle supplies you with:

* Test cases (extend your test classes to `automatically` use their tests):
    * [AbstractWebAppTestCase](https://github.com/GrinWay/web-app-bundle/blob/main/tests/Functional/AbstractWebAppTestCase.php)
* Stimulus controllers:
    * [grinway--transition](https://github.com/GrinWay/web-app-bundle/blob/main/assets/dist/transition.js)
* Twig components:
    * `twig:grinway:Alert`
      [Class](https://github.com/GrinWay/web-app-bundle/blob/main/src/Twig/Component/Alert.php) [Template](https://github.com/GrinWay/web-app-bundle/blob/main/templates/components/Alert.html.twig)
    * `twig:grinway:Flash`
      [Class](https://github.com/GrinWay/web-app-bundle/blob/main/src/Twig/Component/Flash.php) [Template](https://github.com/GrinWay/web-app-bundle/blob/main/templates/components/Flash.html.twig)
